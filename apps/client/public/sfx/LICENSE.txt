Single shots (1911 takes A_34P and A_42P, Smith & Wesson 642 take V_22P) from
"The Free Firearm Sound Library" (Prepared SFX Library), released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), no rights reserved.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Edits: one shot cut from each take, mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz 16-bit,
low-end boost below ~180 Hz, soft-clip limiting for a denser "game" shot,
normalised, 0.35 s fade-out.
